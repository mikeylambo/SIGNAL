Privacy Policy

SIGNAL — Cognitive Calibration Engine · Last updated: 30 July 2026

The short version. SIGNAL has no accounts, no sign-up, no ads, no third-party trackers and no cookies. Your progress lives on your own device.

Nothing is sent to a server unless you choose to post a score to a leaderboard — and even then it is only a nickname you pick, the score, and a random ID. We never ask for your name, email, phone number or location.

You can erase everything the leaderboard holds about you at any time, from inside the game: Settings → Data → Delete my leaderboard data.

1. Who is responsible

SIGNAL is operated by [OPERATOR NAME] ("we", "us"). For anything in this policy, including requests about your data, contact privacy@YOURDOMAIN.com.

2. Stored on your device

SIGNAL saves your game to your browser's local storage. This stays on your device, is not transmitted to us, and is not readable by other websites. We cannot see it.

Clearing your browser's site data for SIGNAL erases all of this permanently. Because there is no account, it cannot be recovered or transferred to another device.

Leaderboards are the only feature that transmits anything you provide, and only at the moment you submit a score. If you never post a score, this section never applies to you.

4. Diagnostics

Some builds of SIGNAL send anonymous crash and usage reports, so we can find out that the game is broken on a device without needing the player to tell us. Where this is enabled you can switch it off in Settings → Access → Diagnostics. It is active by default on those builds; if you would rather it never ran, turn it off and it will send nothing further.

A report contains: a random install ID (deliberately different from your leaderboard Player ID, so diagnostics cannot be matched to a public leaderboard row), your browser's user-agent string, your language setting, your screen pixel ratio and CPU core count, a coarse size bucket (sm/md/lg — never your exact window size, which would be a fingerprinting vector), which events occurred (session start, run start, run end, WebGL failure, error), and for errors a truncated message and stack trace.

It never contains your callsign, your leaderboard IDs, your progress, or any text you typed.

5. What we never collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or postal address.
  • No password — there is nothing to log in to.
  • No GPS or precise location. No IP-based location lookup.
  • No contacts, photos, camera, microphone or file access.
  • No advertising identifiers. No ad networks. No advertising profiles.
  • No cookies, and no third-party analytics, social or tracking scripts.
  • No health or medical data. SIGNAL is a game, not a medical or diagnostic tool, and makes no claim to assess or improve any medical condition.

6. Who else processes it

We do not sell, rent or share your data, and we do not use it for advertising. Two service providers process it on our behalf, only to run the game:

  • Supabase — hosts the leaderboard database.
  • Vercel — serves the game itself. Like any web host it processes your IP address in order to deliver the page and to keep standard server logs; we do not use those logs to build any profile of you.

These providers may store or process data in countries other than your own, including the United States. Where required, such transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

We may also disclose data where the law genuinely requires it.

7. Why we are allowed to hold it (UK/EU GDPR)

  • Leaderboard entries — consent. Nothing is posted unless you choose to post it. You withdraw that consent by deleting your data, as below.
  • Diagnostics — legitimate interest in a game that does not crash, balanced by keeping the data anonymous and giving you an off switch.
  • Reports and bans — legitimate interest in keeping public callsigns free of abusive content.

8. How long it is kept

Leaderboard entries are kept while the board exists, because a leaderboard's purpose is to be a lasting record — or until you delete them. Daily-challenge boards are historical records and are not automatically purged. Reports are kept until reviewed, then closed. Diagnostic reports are retained for up to 90 days. Anything on your device stays until you clear it.

9. Your rights and how to use them

Delete your leaderboard data — immediately, yourself

In the game: Settings → Data → Delete my leaderboard data. This removes your callsign and scores from every leaderboard and forgets the stored identity. It takes effect at once and cannot be undone. Your offline progress, Signal and purchases are kept — clear your browser's site data if you want those gone too.

One exception, stated plainly: if an account has been banned for abusive content, the ban record — a random ID and a moderator note, with nothing you supplied — is kept after erasure. Otherwise deleting your data would double as a way to reset a ban.

Change your callsign

Stats → Change callsign renames you on every board you appear on.

Everything else

Depending on where you live you may also have the right to access, correct, export or restrict use of your data, to object to processing, and not to be subject to automated decision-making (we do not do any). Because SIGNAL holds no account and no contact detail for you, we identify your records by the Player ID your device holds — so please email privacy@YOURDOMAIN.com from a device that has played, and we will explain how to supply it. We reply within 30 days.

If you are in the UK or EU and are unhappy with our response, you may complain to your national data protection authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

California residents: we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined by the CCPA/CPRA, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising any right.

10. Children

SIGNAL is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. The only thing a child could submit is a chosen callsign; if you believe one has been posted, email us and we will remove it. Because a callsign is shown publicly, we would encourage any younger player not to use their real name.

11. Security

Leaderboard traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Writes go through server-side functions that verify your device's ownership secret, so one player cannot alter or delete another's entry. No system is perfectly secure, but note that a breach could not expose a password or an email address, because we never hold either.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. If a change materially affects what we collect or why, we will surface a notice in the game rather than relying on you to re-read this page.
